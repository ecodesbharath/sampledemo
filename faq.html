Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Our friendly team is here to help.

How does Edukron work? +

Edukron is an AI-powered learning platform that personalizes your education journey. Our AI coach analyzes your goals, learning style, and progress to recommend the perfect courses, create custom study plans, and provide real-time feedback.

Do I need any prior experience? +

Not at all! Our courses are designed for beginners and include foundational content. Whether you're switching careers or just starting out, our AI coach will guide you through the learning process step by step.

How long do courses take? +

Course duration varies based on your pace and commitment. Most courses can be completed in 4-12 weeks with 5-10 hours of study per week. Our flexible learning paths allow you to study at your own speed.

Are certificates recognized? +

Yes! Our certificates are industry-recognized and can be shared on LinkedIn. Many employers actively look for Edukron certifications when hiring. Plus, our career coaching helps you leverage these credentials effectively.

Can I cancel my subscription? +

Absolutely. You can cancel your subscription at any time with no penalties. If you cancel during your free trial, you won't be charged. For paid plans, you'll retain access until the end of your billing period.

Do you offer refunds? +

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all paid plans. If you're not satisfied with your learning experience, contact our support team within 30 days for a full refund.

Is there a free trial? +

Yes! Start with our free plan that includes 5 courses, limited AI coaching, and community access. Upgrade to Pro for a 14-day free trial with full access to all features.

Can I access Edukron on mobile? +

Definitely! Edukron is fully responsive and works great on all devices. Download our mobile app for iOS and Android to learn on the go, or access the platform through your browser.
